Rifle & load

On the box or the maker's site. Use G7 if it's listed, since it's more accurate for long, sleek bullets. If not, G1 works fine.

Scope height is from the center of the bore to the center of the scope. It's usually 1.5–2.0". If you're not sure, use 1.5.

Altitude and temperature are on the Shot screen under Conditions.

Before you trust it

Shoot at a known distance of 400 yd or more, dialed to what the app says. If the shots hit high, lower the muzzle velocity by 15–25 fps. If they hit low, raise it. Repeat until the shots land on target. This "truing" step makes the far numbers match your rifle.

Numbers are a starting point and don't replace confirming at the range.
